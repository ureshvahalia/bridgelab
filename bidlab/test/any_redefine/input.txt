## $ANY is reserved; this attempted redefinition should be dropped with a
## warning, not applied. "(Points > 100)" is impossible for any real
## 13-card hand -- if it were mistakenly honored, dealing (which defaults
## to $ANY for N/S here, no positional rule args) could never find a
## matching hand and would exhaust its retry budget instead of completing.
$ANY := (Points > 100);
$.1N. := (15 TO 17 Points);
$.P. := NOT (15 TO 17 Points)
END
